Privacy Policy

RANTEX (“we”, “our”, or “the site”) is a wallpaper discovery website. This policy explains the information that may be processed when you visit or use the site. The site owner must keep this policy accurate as features and service providers change.

Information and site operation

When you visit the site, our hosting and security providers may process technical information needed to deliver and protect the website, such as IP address, browser/device information, requested pages, timestamps and diagnostic logs. RANTEX currently does not require visitors to create an account. Your saved favourites are stored in your own browser using local storage; they are not currently sent to a RANTEX user account.

Images and third-party links

Wallpaper images and other embedded resources may be served by third-party providers. When your browser requests those resources, the provider may receive technical connection information under its own privacy policy. Links to external stores, payment providers and affiliate partners take you to third-party services. Review their policies before submitting information or making a purchase.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

RANTEX may use analytics tools after their identifiers are configured. If Google AdSense is activated after approval, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or similar identifiers to serve, measure and personalise ads, including based on your prior visits to this site or other websites. Third-party vendors, including Google, may place and read cookies on your browser or use web beacons/IP addresses as a result of ad serving on this site.

You can review or manage personalised advertising through Google Ads Settings. You can also review the way Google uses data on partner sites and apps. Browser controls can be used to manage or block cookies, although some site features may not work as intended. Where applicable, RANTEX will provide the consent choices required for visitors in relevant jurisdictions.

Affiliate links and payments

Some product links may be affiliate links. If you follow a marked affiliate link and make a qualifying purchase, RANTEX may earn a commission at no additional cost to you. If paid downloads or memberships are activated, the selected payment provider will process payment details under its own privacy and security terms. RANTEX should not receive or store full card or UPI credentials.

Data retention and contact

Technical log retention is managed by the hosting/security providers according to their terms and settings. Favourites remain in your browser until you clear site data. For privacy questions or requests, contact the site owner using the monitored email address published on the Contact page. The owner must add a real contact email before using the site for business or accepting payments.

Children

RANTEX is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided personal information may contact the site owner.

Policy changes

This policy may be updated when site features, analytics, advertising or payment providers change. Check this page periodically for the current version.
